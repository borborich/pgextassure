# Code of Conduct

PgExtAssure welcomes contributors and users with different backgrounds and
levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Protect private information and follow coordinated disclosure practices.
- Help keep technical discussions focused and accessible.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other participation
that violates this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation.

Report conduct concerns to the repository owner through the contact method on
their GitHub profile. If no private contact method is available, open an issue
containing only the minimum non-sensitive information needed to request private
follow-up.

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, and other
spaces where someone is representing the project.
